# RAGWarrant Contributor License Agreement

Status: `DRAFT — REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW BEFORE ACTIVATION`

This draft is a planning artifact. It is not active, not executed, and not evidence that any current or historical contributor has signed a contributor license agreement.

## Intended Structure

RAGWarrant intends to use a contributor license model in which contributors retain copyright in their contributions while granting the project steward broad rights to use accepted contributions in the Apache-licensed public core and, where legally approved, in commercial offerings.

The final CLA must be reviewed and approved by counsel before activation.

## Individual CLA Placeholder

An individual contributor would grant the project steward and downstream recipients a perpetual, worldwide, nonexclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license to:

- use;
- reproduce;
- prepare derivative works;
- publicly display;
- publicly perform;
- sublicense;
- distribute;
- relicense where legally intended and approved;
- include the contribution in commercial offerings where the contributor grant permits it.

The contributor would represent that they have authority to submit the contribution and that the contribution does not knowingly include secrets, employer-confidential material, proprietary third-party code, or datasets that cannot be contributed under the intended terms.

## Corporate CLA Placeholder

A corporate contributor agreement may be required when an employer or other entity owns the submitted contribution. The corporate form should identify the entity, authorized signer, covered contributors, and scope of authorization.

## Patent License Placeholder

The final CLA should include patent-license treatment consistent with counsel-approved practice and compatible with the Apache-2.0 public core.

## Not Active

Until the CLA mechanism is approved and activated:

- outside contributors are not represented as CLA-covered;
- historical contributors are not represented as having signed a CLA;
- retrospective proprietary relicensing rights are not assumed.
