# RAGWarrant Trademark Policy

Status: `TRADEMARK_OWNERSHIP_PENDING_HUMAN_REVIEW`

RAGWarrant™ names the open-source RAG promotion-governance project. This policy is a governance draft and does not assert a registered trademark or identify a trademark owner because the ownership gate is pending human review.

Apache-2.0 grants copyright and patent permissions described in the license. It does not grant trademark rights.

## Permitted Community Uses

You may use the RAGWarrant name for:

- factual references to the project;
- compatibility statements;
- academic citation;
- unmodified redistribution identification;
- forks that are clearly identified as unofficial and not endorsed by the project steward.

## Prohibited or Improper Uses

Do not use the RAGWarrant name or confusingly similar branding to:

- imply endorsement, sponsorship, certification, or official status without permission;
- name a competing distribution `RAGWarrant Enterprise`;
- brand a fork so that users may confuse it with the official project;
- use official project logos as if sponsored;
- claim federal registration before registration issues.

## Registration Symbol

Do not use the registered-trademark symbol with the RAGWarrant name unless a federal registration has actually issued for the relevant goods or services.

## Questions

Trademark ownership and enforcement authority are pending documented legal review. Until approved, trademark-related decisions should be treated as human/counsel action items.
