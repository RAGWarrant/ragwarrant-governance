# arXiv Paper Plan

Working title: **RAGWarrant: Evidence-Preserving Governance for RAG Policy Promotion Under Quality, Cost, Latency, and Risk Constraints**.

The paper frames RAGWarrant as a systems/methods contribution: a governance and promotion-control layer that consumes evaluator outputs and operational telemetry, then emits auditable promote, block, reject, or inconclusive decisions.

The paper will not claim RAG Compass superiority, human validation, official platform benchmarking, production readiness, hallucination elimination, or broad stable generative governance superiority.
