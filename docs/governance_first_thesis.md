# Governance-First Thesis

RAGWarrant evaluates candidate RAG policies under declared quality, cost, latency, safety, regression, provenance, and statistical requirements. The framework may promote the best eligible method or refuse promotion.

This framing is intentionally different from proving a single optimizer is universally best. In the current evidence base, RAG Compass remains a candidate optimizer, while the stronger result concerns RAGWarrant's governance decision changing a natural CRAG mock-API promotion toward a lower-cost/lower-latency policy.
