# Rightsholder and Steward Record

Status: `PENDING_DOCUMENTED_GOVERNANCE_APPROVAL`

This record separates legal ownership, project stewardship, support operations, and commercial licensing authority. It intentionally does not assert a company rightsholder because the ownership gate is blocked pending human IP review.

| Field | Current repository-supported value | Required evidence before stronger claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copyright owner | `RAGWarrant contributors` | Approved ownership attestation or counsel-approved assignment/license record |
| Trademark owner | `PENDING_HUMAN_REVIEW` | Trademark ownership attestation and clearance review |
| Open-source project steward | `PENDING_DOCUMENTED_GOVERNANCE_APPROVAL` | Maintainer/governance approval |
| Enterprise software publisher | `PENDING_RIGHTS_CONFIRMATION` | Commercial licensing authority and publisher approval |
| Support-services provider | `TO_BE_DESIGNATED` | Approved public support or sales contact |
| Commercial licensing authority | `PENDING_HUMAN_IP_REVIEW` | Assignment, exclusive commercial license, suitable CLA, or other counsel-approved authorization |
| Legal basis for commercial authority | `NOT_DOCUMENTED_IN_REPOSITORY` | Counsel-approved record suitable for public governance metadata |

If the steward or support company differs from the copyright or trademark owner, this repository requires one of the following before describing the steward as having exclusive commercial rights:

- assignment;
- exclusive commercial license;
- suitable contributor/license grant covering the intended commercial use;
- other counsel-approved authorization.
