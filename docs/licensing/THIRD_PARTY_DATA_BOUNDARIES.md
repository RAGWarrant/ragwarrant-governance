# Third Party Data Boundaries

Status: `CRAG_NONCOMMERCIAL_RESEARCH_BOUNDARY_DOCUMENTED`

CRAG is used only for noncommercial research and evaluation. CRAG data are not distributed in this repository.

## Public Research Boundary

- CRAG-derived results are research evidence, not commercial product claims.
- Raw CRAG data, raw CRAG question text, raw source documents, raw prompts, raw generated answers, raw API responses, and secrets must not be committed.
- CRAG-derived results must remain labeled with their evidence and claim boundaries.

## Commercial Firewall

Enterprise functionality must be independently validated using commercially usable evidence, public datasets permitting commercial use, customer-provided evidence under contract, or synthetic fixtures clearly labeled as such.

Do not reclassify noncommercial research evidence as commercial evidence.
