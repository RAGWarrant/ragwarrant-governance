# Historical Evidence Timeline

## 1. Early Simulation And Refusal Discipline

Early SPECTRA / Quantum Fighter-style simulations completed 204 / 204 runs while producing no simulation-supported advantage. Classical baselines dominated, and unsupported advantage claims were refused or marked inconclusive.

## 2. Synthetic RAGWarrant Mechanism Exploration

The synthetic mechanism matrix and calibration passes exercised mechanism logic, refusal controls, regression awareness, cost awareness, and budget parity. This strengthened engineering discipline but did not provide external proof.

## 3. External-Transfer Candidate Signals

External-transfer smoke and expanded transfer runs produced candidate signals for adapter planning, RAG policy, and tool routing. Hallucination/faithfulness, prompt regression, and some curation claims remained inconclusive.

## 4. Matched-Cost And Real-RAG Development

Matched-cost real-RAG development produced a No-Fork/RAG Compass candidate signal, but the certificate was utility fragile. Later evidence narrowed the optimizer-specific interpretation.

## 5. Statistical Audit And Row-Level Reconstruction

Row-level reconstruction showed that an older zero-width CI result was low-information aggregate evidence. The older result was preserved as descriptive candidate-output evidence, not calibrated query-level proof.

## 6. Public End-To-End T2-RAGBench Development

T2-RAGBench development v1 placed RAG Compass near the best point estimate but not superior. Larger v2 was negative for RAG Compass against greedy_regression_aware_search.

## 7. Fresh Public MultiHop Confirmatory Evidence

MultiHop-RAG provided valid public confirmatory evidence. Governance was noninferior, not superior; governed and quality-only selection matched.

## 8. RAGBench HotpotQA Context-Retrieval Enablement

HotpotQA was upgraded to context-retrieval eligibility, enabling policy-dependent context assembly on a second public dataset, but it was not full corpus-backed evidence.

## 9. CRAG Full Corpus-Backed Acquisition

CRAG was acquired under noncommercial research-only restrictions, normalized with streaming all-row processing, and passed hash, leakage, confirmatory-row, and policy-variation gates.

## 10. CRAG Web-Document Governance

CRAG web-document governance was noninferior, not superior: governed and quality-only both selected `top_k_high`.

## 11. CRAG Mock-API Governance Superiority

The strongest current result is CRAG mock-API validation: governance selected `top_k_low` over quality-only `greedy_regression_aware_search`, with held-out confirmatory support under the configured source/retrieval governance utility.

## 12. Natural Beneficial Divergence

Natural divergence adjudication found a mixed signal: 1 beneficial, 4 inconclusive, and 0 harmful cases. This is useful but still limited.

## 13. Unsupported Areas

RAG Compass superiority, generative LLM validation, human evaluation, official external-platform benchmarking, production readiness, broad governance superiority, and legacy hardware/quantum-advantage remain unsupported.

## 14. Current Best Claim

RAGWarrant has its strongest evidence as a governance framework, not as proof of RAG Compass optimizer superiority. The strongest current result is CRAG mock-API governance superiority under source/retrieval governance evaluation, with RAG Compass remaining a secondary candidate optimizer.
