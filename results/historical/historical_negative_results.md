- Classical baselines dominated early Quantum Fighter/SPECTRA-like simulations.
- Early simulations produced no simulation-supported advantage.

- Row-level reconstruction showed the old zero-width CI result was low-information aggregate evidence.
- RAG Compass had no clear niche in later niche analysis.
- RAG Compass ranked 5th in the strongest CRAG mock-API validation.
- Human evaluation was not run.
- No pinned generator was configured.
- Official platform integrations were not run.
